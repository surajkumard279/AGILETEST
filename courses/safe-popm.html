Description

About the course

Learn how product owners and product managers plan, prioritize and deliver value on an Agile Release Train.

Who will benefit

  • Product owners
  • Product managers
  • Business owners and business analysts

Topics covered

  • Customer-centric product thinking
  • Building and refining the backlog
  • Preparing for PI planning
  • Supporting iteration execution
  • Delivering and measuring value
  • Working with stakeholders

Prerequisites

  • Experience with product or business analysis work
  • Awareness of Scrum and Kanban

What you will learn

  • Connect team backlogs to program goals
  • Prioritize by economic value
  • Take part in PI planning with confidence
  • Judge whether delivered work met its goal

What you get

  • Live virtual class with hands-on group activities
  • Course workbook and templates
  • Certificate of attendance
  • Access to a learner community after class
