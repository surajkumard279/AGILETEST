Description

About the course

Learn a four-step way to design classes where learners connect, do, share and reflect instead of listening to lectures.

Who will benefit

  • Trainers and facilitators
  • Agile coaches
  • Team leads who teach

Topics covered

  • Why people learn more by doing
  • The four steps of a learner-led session
  • Designing activities that stick
  • Working in a virtual room
  • Giving instructions people can follow
  • Redesigning one of your own sessions

Prerequisites

  • Everyone is welcome, whatever their experience
  • Some familiarity with Agile ideas helps

What you will learn

  • Design a short learner-led session
  • Replace a lecture with an activity
  • Run activities well online
  • Get useful feedback from learners

What you get

  • Live virtual class with hands-on group activities
  • Course workbook and templates
  • Certificate of attendance
  • Access to a learner community after class
